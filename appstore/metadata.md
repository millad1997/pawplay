**Promotional text** (159/170)

> Lay your iPhone or iPad flat and watch your cat pounce. Three games, gentle sounds, and a pause button paws can't press. Free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

**Description**

> Pounce Party turns your iPhone or iPad into a toy your cat can actually play with. Fish, mice and butterflies dart, freeze and wander across the screen like real prey. When a paw lands on one, it's caught with a splash, puff or sparkle, and a new one comes in from the edge.
>
> THREE GAMES
> • Fish Pond: a golden koi glides and darts around the lily pads.
> • Mouse Chase: a little mouse scurries along the floor, freezes, then bolts.
> • Butterfly Garden: a blue butterfly flutters over the grass and lands on flowers.
>
> MADE FOR CATS
> • Movement that switches between darting, freezing and wandering, the way prey does
> • Bright blues and yellows, the colours cats see best
> • A generous touch area, because paws aren't precise
> • Optional sounds: bubbles, squeaks and chimes
>
> MADE FOR OWNERS
> • Set the speed, target size and session length
> • A pause button that must be held for 2 seconds, so a paw can't stop the game
> • See how many targets your cat caught and how long they played
> • Remembers your settings
> • Works offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no data collected
>
> Tip: turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access) to keep your cat inside the game.
>
> Always supervise your cat while it plays. A screen protector is a good idea for cats who play with their claws out.

**Notes:**

> Pounce Party is a game for cats: the owner sets it up, then lays the device flat for the cat to touch moving targets.
>
> To review: choose any game, press Start, and wait for the 3-second countdown. Tap the moving fish, mouse or butterfly to catch it; a new one appears.
>
> To pause: press and HOLD the round pause button in the top-right corner for 2 seconds. Short taps are deliberately ignored so a cat can't pause the game. From the pause screen, choose Resume or Finish.
>
> All game content is bundled in the app and works offline. The app loads no remote website, needs no account, and collects no data.
