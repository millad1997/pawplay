Getting started

  1. Choose Fish Pond, Mouse Chase or Butterfly Garden.
  2. Pick a speed, target size, session length and sound.
  3. Press Start and lay the device flat beside your cat.
  4. When your cat touches a target, it's caught and a new one appears.

Common questions

How do I pause or stop?

Hold the pause button in the top corner for 2 seconds. A quick tap won't pause, so your cat can't stop the game by accident. From the pause screen you can resume or finish.

How do I keep my cat from leaving the game?

Use Guided Access: go to Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access and turn it on. Open Pounce Party, then triple-click the side (or Home) button to start it. Triple-click again to end it.

My cat isn't interested.

Every cat is different. Try another game, Slow speed, or Large targets, and turn the sound on. Short sessions when your cat is curious and playful work best.

There's no sound.

Check that Sound is on in the game settings and that your ringer switch isn't on silent. Sound follows the ringer switch.

Will claws scratch my screen?

Most cats tap with soft paws, but a screen protector is a good idea. Stay nearby while your cat plays.
