Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Pounce Party is a touch game for cats. It is built to work without collecting any information about you, your cat, or your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Pounce Party has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The iPhone and iPad app makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

Your game settings (speed, target size, session length, sound) and the result of your most recent session are saved on your device so the game remembers them. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing website data in your browser, removes them.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive or use that information.

Children

Pounce Party does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Questions? See the support page for how to reach us.
