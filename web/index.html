Playing with your cat

  1. Lay the device flat on the floor or a table beside your cat, then press Start. Stay nearby while they play.
  2. Protect the screen. A screen protector helps if your cat plays with claws out.
  3. Keep the cat in the game with Guided Access: Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access. Turn it on, then triple-click the side (or Home) button while Pawplay is open.
  4. Add Pawplay to your Home Screen so it opens full-screen: tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
  5. To pause, hold the ⏸ button in the corner for 2 seconds. A quick paw tap won't stop the game.
  6. Not interested? Try another game, Slow speed, or Large targets. Sound follows your ringer switch.
